Malaysia · Input Costs
RHB just told planters to brace for a stronger CPO run — Mamee's cooking-oil bill is next
RHB Research raised its 2027 and 2028 crude palm oil price forecasts to RM4,500 and RM4,400 a tonne respectively (from RM4,300), citing a high probability of a strong El Niño hitting from October 2026 onwards. Current CPO already trades at RM4,600–4,700 a tonne — above RHB's own RM4,400 assumption for this year. Malaysian Palm Oil Board agronomists expect fresh fruit bunch yields to fall 10–14% in the event's first year, concentrated in Johor, Pahang, Negeri Sembilan and Sabah, the same belt that feeds the country's frying-oil supply chain.
Mamee angle: palm oil is a direct input across the fried-snack and instant-noodle portfolio. A multi-year CPO uptrend argues for locking in forward contracts now and testing smaller pack/price architecture ahead of Q4 — before the El Niño effect shows up in supplier quotes from October.
