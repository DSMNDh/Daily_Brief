FMCG — Product Innovation
A Thai functional-snack brand just turned a bag of chips into a protein claim — and put a K-pop-adjacent face on the pack
Plantae, a Thai plant-nutrition brand best known for supplements, launched Plantae Plant-Protein Chips this week in a collaboration with Thai singer-actor Daou Pittaya. Made from yellow and green peas blended with jasmine rice, each pack delivers 7g of protein — about one egg's worth — for 120 calories, and is oven-baked rather than fried. The range comes in three flavours (Sour Cream, Cheesy BBQ, Nori Seaweed), is sugar-free and low-sodium, and sells for just THB25 (roughly RM3) nationwide at 7-Eleven. It's the latest step in Plantae's push from supplements into everyday snacking occasions, alongside protein bars and vitamin-spiked soda.
Mamee angle: functional/protein snacking is still white space for Mister Potato and Corntoz — and Plantae just proved the claim doesn't need premium pricing or a big-name celebrity budget to work. A limited-run "protein chip" SKU fronted by a regional micro-to-mid creator, priced at convenience-store impulse level, could stake out this territory before Lay's or Pringles formalise a regional protein play.
