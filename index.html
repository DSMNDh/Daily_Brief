Nestlé turns a 1960s roadside dessert into ready-to-drink coffee — and hands Mamee a nostalgia playbook it already owns
Nestlé Malaysia has launched Nescafé Ais Kepal, a limited-edition ready-to-drink coffee that reimagines ais kepal — the shaved-ice-and-syrup treat sold at roadside stalls in the 1950s and 60s — in bottled format. It's the latest entry in a run of specifically-Malaysian nostalgia NPD this month, following NIMS' teh tarik and sirap bandung chocolate-snack tie-in earlier in the cycle. Mamee angle: Mamee already owns nostalgia equity most challengers don't — Mister Potato, Mamee Monster's 20-plus-year run with a generation of Malaysian schoolkids, the recess-era snacking ritual itself. A limited-edition SKU built around a specific, dated Mamee childhood memory would compete directly in the lane Nestlé just proved works, before more competitors pile in.
