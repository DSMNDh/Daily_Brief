Fuel prices step up again this week — and Malaysia's snack aisle will feel it before shoppers do
Diesel, RON97 and unsubsidised RON95 all rose by RM0.35 a litre for the 17-23 September pricing week, the latest move in a run-up tied to Brent crude trading near US$104 a barrel as tensions in West Asia persist. Unsubsidised RON95 now retails at RM4.37 and diesel at RM5.27, even as more than 16 million BUDI Madani recipients stay shielded at RM1.99 and RM2.10 respectively. For FMCG makers, the pass-through isn't really at the pump — it's in the delivery van and the packaging film: logistics, palm-oil-linked inputs and freight all move with the same crude curve, whether or not a brand's own customers ever see a fuel receipt.
Mamee angle: this is a margin story before it's a marketing story. Logistics and flexible-packaging costs move with the same crude curve as pump prices — worth stress-testing Q4 pack pricing and promo mechanics now, before shrinkflation becomes the story shoppers tell instead of the brand.
