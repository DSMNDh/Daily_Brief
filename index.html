Retail Pressure
A Chinese shopping app just showed Malaysians a pair of slippers for RM3.50 — and put snack aisles on notice
Pinduoduo has been live in Malaysia for only nine months but has already rattled Shopee, TikTok Shop and Lazada, according to The Edge Malaysia's latest cover story. Its team-buy pricing produces startling gaps — a pair of slippers priced at RM16 in stores sells for RM3.50 on the app, a 78% discount — and Touch 'n Go eWallet integration removed the last friction point for Malaysian shoppers. Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim acknowledged the pressure just last week after officiating TikTok Shop Summit 2026, flagging foreign operators renting Port Klang warehouses with no local job creation, while MICCI, ACCCIM and the Malaysian Retail Chain Association push for a level playing field ahead of a proposed E-Commerce Bill.
Mamee angle: perishable, locally-manufactured snacks are harder for cross-border platforms to undercut than durable goods — 15–30 day sea-freight lead times work against fresh food — but the price-anchoring effect still trains shoppers to expect steep discounts everywhere they shop. Leaning harder into owned TikTok Shop and Shopee storefronts, with a visible "made fresh, delivered fast" message, defends against that spillover before it reaches the snack aisle.
