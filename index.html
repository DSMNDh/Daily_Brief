F&N's profit rose 10% this quarter — but only because costs fell, not because Malaysians bought more
Fraser & Neave Holdings posted a 10.3% jump in Q3 FY2026 net profit to RM93.58 million, published yesterday evening. The gain came entirely from lower tax and operating expenses — quarterly revenue actually fell 3.8% to RM1.2 billion, dragged by an 18.4% F&B Indochina slump from prolonged border closures, even as its Malaysian beverage and dairy lines grew 8.4% on stronger export sales and marketing. F&N's own outlook statement is the real story for Mamee: management said it "may resort to price hikes... as a last resort," citing cost pressure it is currently absorbing through trade-spend discipline and supply-chain optimisation rather than passing straight to shelf price.
Mamee angle: If Malaysia's most conservative major F&B player is publicly flagging price hikes as a live option while still growing export volume, the whole category's pricing ceiling is being tested in public — worth benchmarking Mamee's own trade-spend and export mix against F&N's playbook before deciding whether Q4 needs a hold-the-line stance or a quiet SKU-level adjustment.
