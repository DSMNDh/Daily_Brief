FMCG SNACK — MALAYSIA
A health-food restaurant chain just skipped the retail queue and put chips straight on Jaya Grocer's shelves
Agrain Holdings, known for its healthy-food restaurants in Kuala Lumpur and Petaling Jaya, has launched packaged chips — Taro, Pumpkin, Banana and Purple Sweet Potato — under the "Grab Signatures" co-brand, sold through Jaya Grocer's retail network and delivered via GrabMart. The 45–60g packs are priced at RM20.90 and lean hard on high-fibre, cholesterol-free, no-added-colouring claims. It's the second foodservice-to-retail snack launch from the Grab Signatures programme this week, alongside Beautea's whole-tea-leaf range, suggesting Grab is actively recruiting F&B brands into a private-label-style snack aisle play.
Mamee angle: this is a new competitive vector — not a rival snack major, but foodservice brands using GrabMart/Jaya Grocer as an instant retail shelf at premium price points (RM20.90 for ~50g is a health-halo premium play, not a value one). Worth watching whether "clean label" claims start pulling share from mainstream salty snacks at the healthy end, and worth testing whether Mamee's own lighter sub-lines could pilot the same quick-commerce co-branded shelf model to trial formats without full retail-listing risk.
