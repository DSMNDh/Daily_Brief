SIAL Malaysia's 2026 innovation finalists put protein and functional formats ahead of fried snacks
Food & Drinks Malaysia by SIAL 2026 runs its closing day today at MITEC Kuala Lumpur (21–23 July), and the ten finalists named for its Innovation Awards lean hard into better-for-you and functional formats: Milkkin's whey-protein snack, Grusonn Wellness's Vive Chocolate (a cocoa drink mix spiked with functional mushrooms), Lemon Me Farm's snack roll made from fruit by-products, and Pakdee's flour-free noodle range from Thailand. Not one shortlisted product is a traditional fried chip. The show has drawn more than 18,000 trade visitors and 600-plus exhibitors from 60 countries, running alongside the ASEAN Barista Team Championship's 18-team regional final.
Mamee angle: the region's innovation bar has shifted to protein, upcycled ingredients and functional add-ins rather than flavour variants alone. Worth benchmarking the current NPD pipeline against these formats before the next cycle, and scouting finalists like Milkkin or Grusonn as co-manufacturing or ingredient leads rather than only watching from the aisle.
