Mister Potato and KFC just ran Mamee's biggest Merdeka-season collab — and teased it like a heist film
KFC Malaysia and Mister Potato launched their first-ever tie-up, "Gabungan Terangup" ("united crunch"), built around two limited-time products — a Spicy Potato Crisp Burger and Mister Potato Syok Wave x KFC Cheezy Wedges chips (nationwide from early September).
