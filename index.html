Malaysia Policy
Fuel budget just got 50% bigger for 16 million Malaysians — right as snack aisles need the traffic
From today, the government restores the monthly BUDI MADANI RON95 quota from 200 to 300 litres and lifts the BUDI Diesel allocation to as much as 400 litres, reversing April's temporary cut. PM Anwar Ibrahim announced the reversal in his 30 August National Day address; the Ministry of Finance says roughly 16 million subsidised users benefit, citing improved fuel-supply management, stronger enforcement and a healthier fiscal position. It's the second consecutive month of household-facing relief after the National Day speech's "shared prosperity" framing.
Mamee angle: this restores real spending room in exactly the price-sensitive households driving 2026's rare value-growth cycle in snacks. It's modest — a few extra tanks of petrol, not a windfall — but it lands right as Merdeka-season promo spend cools and before the 9.9 e-commerce sale. Worth flagging to trade marketing ahead of month-end multi-pack and sundry-shop promo planning.
