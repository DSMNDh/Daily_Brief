Malaysia Retail
7-Eleven Malaysia's profit halves as store expansion outruns margins — even as revenue and same-store sales keep climbing
7-Eleven Malaysia's 2QFY2026 net profit plunged 58.5% to RM8.28mil, even as revenue rose 9.7% to RM883.61mil, after a net 125 new stores in H1 (taking the network to 2,776 outlets, including 1,010 CAFé-format stores, up 355 YoY) pushed operating costs RM16mil higher. Management says the drag is time-bound: same-store sales growth is improving, average transaction size is trending above last year, and profit should recover in H2 — but it flagged subsidy-rationalisation pass-through and inflation as headwinds to watch for the rest of the year. No dividend was declared for the quarter.
This matters for Mamee because 7-Eleven is Malaysia's largest single convenience-store distribution channel for packaged snacks and instant noodles — its shelf economics ripple straight through to trade terms.
Mamee angle: as CAFé conversion keeps eating into classic-store shelf space for fresh food and drinks, and 7-Eleven's own management signals it's "calibrating pricing and promotional activity to remain competitive while safeguarding margins," expect convenience-channel trade-support asks to tighten into H2. Use the current same-store-sales recovery narrative to lock in end-cap and multi-buy placements before that squeeze lands — Merdeka Month is the last easy window to do it.
