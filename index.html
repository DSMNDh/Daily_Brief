MAHA 2026 (Malaysia Agriculture, Horticulture and Agrotourism Exhibition), running 28 August–6 September at MAEPS Serdang under the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security's "Value Creation for Food Security" banner, enters its final full day today before closing tomorrow.
