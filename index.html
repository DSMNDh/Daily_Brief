Malaysia's detailed Q2 GDP release today confirms retail trade, not exports, is carrying consumer momentum
DOSM published its detailed, preliminary Q2 2026 GDP breakdown today, following last month's 5.8% advance estimate. The report shows the services sector's revenue climbing 11.2% year-on-year to RM714.7bil, while separately released wholesale and retail trade data for May put total sales up 11% YoY to RM171.3bil — wholesale trade alone jumped 18.4% to RM80.8bil. DOSM explicitly credits higher household spending and mobility during festive celebrations and the mid-year school holidays for the boost across wholesale and retail trade, food and beverage, accommodation and transport sub-sectors. For snack and FMCG brands, it's a clearer read than the headline GDP number: Malaysian households kept spending on occasion-driven, non-essential categories through Q2 even as the value-shift narrative dominates the broader conversation.
Mamee angle: the festive and school-holiday spending bump DOSM just confirmed is exactly the discretionary, occasion-led purchasing snacks compete for. Use the next two weeks — before momentum cools into September — to push in-store and TikTok Shop bundle promos tied to weekend outings and back-to-school moments rather than leading with pure price-value messaging.
