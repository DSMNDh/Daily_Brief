Malaysia's Merdeka season quietly opens — and rivals are already three campaigns deep
As August opens, Sony Malaysia has launched its "Moments With Sony" Merdeka campaign (running 31 Jul-4 Oct across BRAVIA TVs, Alpha cameras and audio products), Grab has rolled out a 2026 Merdeka music-video campaign spotlighting delivery riders and small sellers, and Duopharma's Flavettes tied a Merdeka push to Tourism Malaysia with limited-edition Kapten Batik merchandise. All three landed in the same week, nearly a month before the actual 31 August public holiday. For FMCG and snack brands, Merdeka remains one of the few nationalistic moments that carries almost no political risk, which is exactly why early movers are locking in media inventory and retail tie-ins this far out.
Mamee angle: if a Merdeka activation isn't already briefed, the runway is narrowing — Sony and Grab are already weeks into their campaign windows.
