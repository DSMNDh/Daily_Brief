7-Eleven turns Michelin-recognised recipes into a RM15.90 ready meal
Today, CAFÉ by 7-Eleven launched "Santap Lah," a ready-to-eat rice-dish line co-created with chefs from four Michelin Guide-recognised Malaysian restaurants — Jawi House, Beta KL, akar and Restaurant au Jardin. Each halal-certified, preservative-free dish sells for RM15.90 and is served hot at the counter, reframing convenience retail as a channel for culinary prestige rather than just packaged snacks. It's a rare case of fine-dining IP being licensed directly into mass grab-and-go retail through one of Malaysia's largest convenience-store networks.
Mamee angle: convenience retail is proving itself a premiumisation channel, not just a discount aisle. A chef-collaboration or "elevated heritage flavour" capsule range — co-branded with a known local chef or restaurant and sold through convenience-store formats — could let a Mamee snack line borrow the same culinary prestige at snack price points rather than RM15.90 ready-meal pricing.
