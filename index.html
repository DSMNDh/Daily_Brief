AmInvestment Bank Research flagged Eco-Shop Bhd as the most direct listed beneficiary of a hardening bargain-hunting trend among Malaysian shoppers, who are now price-benchmarking across channels — including Shopee and TikTok Shop — as households shift to a defensive, needs-based basket.
