FMCG / QSR — Merdeka Season
Kopitiam nostalgia becomes Merdeka's flavour battleground, as two unrelated chains launch heritage collabs on the same day
On 20 August, Tim Hortons Malaysia and FamilyMart Malaysia both published Merdeka-season collaborations built on the exact same idea within hours of each other: pairing a modern format with a beloved local kopitiam brand. Tim Hortons teamed up with Hock Kee Kopitiam on a "Sandwic Ayam Sambal," launching nationwide today, while FamilyMart paired with Oriental Kopi on "Rasa Kopitiam Bestnya Lokal." Neither chain coordinated with the other — the overlap signals that genuine local-heritage partnership, not red-and-white packaging, has become the default National Month activation format across categories, from QSR to convenience retail.
Mamee angle: a Mamee-family brand x kopitiam, mamak or local-icon flavour or pack collab timed before 31 August would ride the same current — and stands out precisely because it isn't just seasonal packaging. Own-channel distribution across TikTok, IG and FB (as Grab and Nutriplus are both doing this week) should carry it rather than a single-platform push.
