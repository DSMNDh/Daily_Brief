Budget 2027 lands tomorrow carrying a RM17 billion consumption lever — and the snack aisle sits directly downstream of it
Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim tables Budget 2027 in the Dewan Rakyat on Friday 9 October, in what is widely read as his government's last budget before a general election.
