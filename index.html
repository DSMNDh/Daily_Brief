Global FMCG
Global snack giants are growing faster abroad than at home — and Southeast Asia is exactly where that growth is coming from
Q2 2026 results from PepsiCo, Mondelez, Kraft Heinz and Hershey all point the same way: a cautious, price-sensitive shopper in the US, and a faster-growing customer base in Latin America, India and Southeast Asia. Mondelez's Asia, Middle East and Africa business grew 8.2% organically in the quarter, and PepsiCo's Asia Pacific Foods revenue climbed 12%, even as North America growth stalled or declined outright for most of these majors. For a category already crowded with regional and local players, that's a signal the world's biggest snack companies are about to lean harder into Southeast Asian shelf space and distribution to offset a saturated home market.
Mamee angle: as Frito-Lay, Oreo and other MNC portfolios chase growth outside the US by pushing deeper into SEA distribution, expect sharper competition for modern-trade shelf space and media share of voice here. The defence isn't matching their spend — it's leaning harder into hyperlocal flavour, nostalgia and price-tier plays that a global portfolio brand can't easily replicate market by market.
