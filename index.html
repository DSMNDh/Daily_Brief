Oil jumps 3% on a Houthi strike on Saudi Arabia — and Malaysia's snack aisle will feel the pass-through first
Brent crude settled at US$106.60 a barrel today, up US$3.52 (3.4%), its highest close in about a week, after a Houthi missile attack on Saudi Arabia reignited Gulf supply-risk fears; WTI rose a similar 2.7% to US$94.61. Mamee angle: lock in packaging-film and freight contracts now rather than waiting for a domestic diesel/RON97 price step-up to confirm the trend, and stress-test promo-heavy SKUs (multi-packs, bundle deals) that have the least room to absorb another cost shock before Budget 2027 on 9 October.
