Travel Retail & Tourism
Thailand halves its visa-free window to 30 days today — and Malaysia's land-border shoppers are the one group walking straight through
From today, Thailand replaces its 60-day visa exemption with a 30-day (and in some cases 15-day) window for nationals of 60 countries, including the US, UK, Canada and Australia, per the Tourism Authority of Thailand. Most trips already run 7–10 days, so the near-term tourist-flow hit looks limited, but longer-haul and backpacker segments — the visitors who linger longest in modern trade and travel retail — are the ones most affected. Crucially, Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and Singapore are explicitly carved out of the new twice-a-year land-border cap that accompanies the reform, meaning Malaysia-Thailand land crossings keep their current freedom of movement even as broader long-haul travel-retail dwell time in Thailand tightens.
Mamee angle: shorter long-haul stays mean less in-country dwell time for impulse snack exposure in Thai duty-free and modern trade — while the untouched MY-TH land corridor (Padang Besar, Bukit Kayu Hitam, Rantau Panjang) becomes the comparatively more resilient cross-border channel. Worth pushing grab-and-go, single-serve formats into Thai modern trade ahead of the year-end travel season, and keeping land-border wholesalers well stocked while that channel holds its advantage.
