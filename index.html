Global FMCG — Ingredient Transparency
Beco's ingredient-callout ad war on Surf Excel and Vim signals a new FMCG battleground: what's actually inside the pack
Sustainable homecare brand Beco (India) launched #WarOnWhatsHidden on 17 August, a comparative campaign built around independent lab tests naming Benzisothiazolinone (BIT) and Linear Alkylbenzene Sulfonate (LAS) as ingredients detected in Hindustan Unilever's Surf Excel Matic Liquid and Vim Liquid. Conceptualised by independent agency Colloquial, the two films skip the usual performance claims entirely and ask consumers to read past front-of-pack marketing to the formulation underneath. It's a direct, branded attack on a category leader's credibility, not just its market share.
Mamee angle: comparative, ingredient-led attacks are migrating from homecare into food and snacking as clean-label scrutiny grows across Malaysia and Thailand. Mister Potato and Mamee Monster's own MSG, preservative and oil-quality claims should be audit-ready — and there's a real opening to lead on formulation transparency before a challenger brand frames that conversation first.
