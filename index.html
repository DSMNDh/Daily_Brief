The FIFA World Cup 2026 final landed at 4AM MYT today, closing a 39-day tournament that economists estimate injected up to RM2.1 billion into Malaysia's F&B sector. The gains were concentrated in the 9pm–3am window: mamak eateries and casual dining logged 15-30% revenue growth on group late-night viewing, food delivery platforms saw 15-35% order growth, and digital commerce (e-wallets, cross-border payments) spiked alongside it. Mamee angle: treat the next 10-14 days as a "habit hangover" window, not a dead zone.
